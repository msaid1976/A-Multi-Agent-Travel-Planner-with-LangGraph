About this application

TripMate AI

TripMate AI is a multi-agent travel planner that brings flights, stays, and destination experiences into one clear, personalised itinerary. Share your travel idea and receive a practical plan designed around your journey.

Its specialised AI agents research travel details and organise the results into a thoughtful response. Your chosen name and avatar stay in your browser, making each planning session feel personal.
